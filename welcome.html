Welcome to Page Pilot

Page Pilot scans any page and speaks a concise summary of what you can do.

Quick start

  1. Pin the extension to your toolbar.
  2. Open any webpage and click the Page Pilot icon.
  3. Click Scan Page (or press Ctrl+Enter).
  4. Open Settings and paste your Gemini API key for AI summaries.

Get a free key at Google AI Studio.
